# Step 12.1: Farm art swap (user's Fantasy Farm pack)

## Goal
Replace the code-drawn farm with the user's own art, and keep the existing plants unchanged.

## What was built
2026-11-24:
- **Art pipeline:** `tools/art/build_farm_pack.py` copies the art into `app/assets/sprites/farm/`.
  - House and tree are shown at half size (box-downscaled with hard alpha), because they're drawn for a bigger world.
  - Grass, dirt path, water and soil tiles, the bush, flowers, stones and tufts are used at native size.
- **Layout** (`FarmLayout`, base 192×320):
  - The farmhouse is centred on top and **doubles as the barn**: tap it for storage.
  - A tree on the left and a bush on the right.
  - A dirt path runs from the door along the right edge.
  - Then the two soil plots, the pond, and the 3 decoration spots.
  - The old barn and cottage are no longer drawn. Crops are unchanged.
- **Full-screen fit:** the farm grows to fill the space between the header and "Next up". The extra height is spread between the house, plot A, plot B and the pond row, and grass tiles fill the side margins.
- **Tests:** layout hit tests updated (house = barn, deco spot moved). **148 tests pass.**
- **Checked on the emulator** (`docs/design/screens/phase12-farm-art.png`).
