# Step 9.2: Settings & daily reminder

## Settings (the section below Profile)
| Setting | Control | Default | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day resets at** | Choice: midnight, 2, 3, **4**, 5 or 6 AM | 4 AM | The farm day boundary (rollover, dormancy, journal days). The farm and Progress screens update immediately |
| **Daily reminder** | Switch plus a time chip (opens the time picker) | **Off** until the player turns it on (or Onboarding asks, Phase 10) | One cozy notification a day: "Your sprouts are waiting. Plant or tend one thing today." Tapping it opens the Farm |
| **Sound** | Switch | On | Stored now; sound effects arrive in Phase 10 |
| **Haptics** | Switch | On | All the app's vibrations go through one helper that checks this setting |
| **Reduce motion** | Switch | Off (the system setting also applies) | Forces Reduce Motion app-wide: no coin flight or particles, the capybara stays still |

## Reminder mechanics
- When the reminder is turned **on**, the app asks for notification permission first. If it's denied, the switch turns back off with an explanation.
- It's scheduled with `zonedSchedule` plus `matchDateTimeComponents: time`, so it repeats daily at that time. It uses inexact timing, so it needs no exact-alarm permission.
- **Changing the time** reschedules it. **Turning it off** cancels it (fixed id `990001`).
- **Time zone:** the next occurrence is worked out in the phone's local time and converted to UTC. Repeating at a fixed UTC time is exact for zones without daylight saving, such as India. Where clocks change, it may shift by an hour; we'll revisit with full zone support later.

## App-wide preferences
- `appPrefsProvider` streams haptics, sound and reduce-motion from Settings.
- `MaterialApp.builder` turns on `disableAnimations` when **Reduce motion** is on.
- `core/feedback.dart`: `AppFeedback.light()` and `.medium()` check the haptics preference. Every direct `HapticFeedback` call is replaced with these.
