# Step 2.4: Player avatars (8 animals × 3 colours)

## Goal
Give players a personal avatar. Per the founder's rule, **the capybara is never offered**; it stays the farm's mascot NPC.

## Design
- **8 animals:** fox, rabbit, cat, dog, frog, bear, duck, owl.
- **3 colour variants each:**
  - fox: red / arctic / silver
  - rabbit: grey / snow / brown
  - cat: ginger / grey / black
  - dog: golden / brown / dalmatian-white
  - frog: green / teal / gold
  - bear: brown / black / polar
  - duck: yellow / white / mallard-brown
  - owl: barn / grey / snowy
- **Format:** 16×16 front-facing portraits in the same style as the other sprites: warm dark outline, light from the top-left, muted ramps. Each animal gets its own silhouette features: pointed, tall, floppy or round ears; muzzle; bill or beak; frog eyes; owl tufts.
- **Files:** `app/assets/sprites/cultiva/avatars/<animal>_<0-2>.png` (24 images) and a preview sheet at `docs/design/art/avatars.png`.
- **Flutter:**
  - `domain/avatar.dart`: a pure `Avatar(animal, variant)` value with `id` (e.g. `fox_0`) and `parse()` that falls back safely.
  - `core/ui/avatar_portrait.dart`: `AvatarPortrait`, drawn at 2 dp per pixel with nearest-neighbour scaling.
  - The Farm header shows the default avatar (`fox_0`) until Profile (Phase 9) lets the player choose. The capybara isn't in the enum, so it can't be picked.

## How we verify it
- The preview sheet shows all 24 portraits, each readable at 1× and recognisable as its animal.
- Unit tests: `Avatar.parse` round-trips every id, rejects unknown or `capybara` ids by falling back to the default, and the list of animals contains no capybara.
