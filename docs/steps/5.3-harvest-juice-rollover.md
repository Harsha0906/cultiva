# Step 5.3: Harvest juice & 4 AM rollover

## Harvest feedback (Farm screen)
- **Coin fly:** 5 coin sprites arc from the harvested plant to the header's coin icon, staggered 60 ms, about 600 ms each, easing out. The coin pill **bumps** (scale 1 → 1.15 → 1) when they land.
- **Leaf burst:** the Flame scene spawns 6 leaf particles and a dust puff at the plant. They rise, drift and fade over 0.7 s.
- **Haptics:** medium on harvest, plus an extra light tap when the Full Basket bonus pays.
- **Reduce Motion:** no flying coins or particles. The pill number simply updates.

## Rollover (from step 3.2's note)
- When the farm view loads, any **ripe but unharvested** completion from an *earlier* farm day is harvested automatically, using **that day's** cap and Full Basket rules. Coins aren't lost because the player forgot to tap.
- Tasks that were removed are skipped.
- A finished to-do is archived, as with a normal harvest.
- **Idempotent:** a second run finds nothing to do, so the change stream settles.
- `FarmService.harvest` is refactored into `_harvestOn(task, day)`, which both paths share.

## Verification
- Service tests:
  - a ripe-yesterday plant is auto-harvested into yesterday's ledger
  - yesterday's cap is respected
  - running rollover twice pays once
  - removed tasks are skipped
- Manual check on the emulator: coins fly to the pill and leaves burst.
