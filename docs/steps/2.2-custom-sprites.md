# Step 2.2: Custom Cultiva sprites (16px density)

## Goal
Draw everything the farm needs that the packs don't have, in a style that sits naturally next to CraftPix art.

## Style rules (sampled from the packs)
- **Outlines:** dark and warm (#482a2a / #46332a / #443734), on every side, never black.
- **Light:** comes from the top-left.
- **Colours:** muted. Greens #275f3e → #96c55f, reds #6d1924 → #9e3931, creams #c1ab93 → #e7dcc4, slates #565a65 → #92a9b6.
- **Density:** 1 px = 1 game pixel on the 16×16 grid. No sub-pixel detail.

## Sprites
| Sprite | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `buildings/cottage_teal.png` | 80×80 | Home. Teal hip roof, round-window dormer, terracotta chimney, cream timber walls, arched door, **capybara sign**, flower boxes |

| `npc/capybara.png` | 6 frames × 20×16 | Mascot NPC, facing right (the game flips it for left): idle ×2, walk ×4. A leaf sprout on its head is the mascot's signature. **NPC only, never an avatar** |

| `tiles/pond.png` | 48×48 9-slice | Water with a sandy rim and highlights |
| `fx/sparkle.png` | 4 × 9×9 | "Ripe" sparkle |
| `fx/coin.png` | 4 × 10×10 | Spinning coin (HUD and harvest) |
| `fx/dust.png` | 3 × 10×8 | Planting and harvest puff |
| `fx/leaf.png` | 2 × 5×5 | Harvest particles |

**Output:** `app/assets/sprites/cultiva/`. This is our own art, so it's committed. A preview sheet goes to `docs/design/art/cultiva-sprites.png`.

## Implementation
`tools/art/draw_cultiva16.py` holds the helpers: shaded ellipse, rectangles, uniform warm outline pass, and 9-slice export. It then draws each sprite.

## How we verify it
- A preview places the cottage and barn next to pack trees and crops on the grass base.
- The capybara animation strip is checked frame by frame.
- 9-slices are tested by stretching them to 3× size, looking for seams.
