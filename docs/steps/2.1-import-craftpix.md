# Step 2.1: Import CraftPix packs

## Goal
Turn the two CraftPix sheets into **named, game-ready sprites** that the app loads, without ever copying the raw packs wholesale.

## Design
- **Script:** `tools/art/build_pack_assets.py` reads the packs from `E:\` and writes only the sprites we use to `app/assets/sprites/pack/`.
  - That folder is **git-ignored**, because the CraftPix license forbids redistributing source files, so the repo never contains pack pixels.
  - Anyone building the app re-runs the script with the packs on disk.
- **Crops:** 9 crops from the plants pack: grape, bean, pepper, cauliflower, peas, lettuce, squash, pumpkin, wheat.
  - Each becomes a strip `crops/<name>.png` of **5 frames**: `seed · sprout · young · ripe · dormant`.
  - Frames are 32 px wide and the row height, bottom-aligned on the pack's soil baseline.
  - `seed` is the pack's "mound with seed" item. `sprout`, `young` and `ripe` are pack columns 0–2.
  - **`dormant` is generated:** the young plant recoloured to a dry olive and tan. That's a modification, which the license allows.
- **House pack:** trees, bushes, rocks, fences, scarecrow, well, mushrooms, crates, stumps, flowers and the red house.
  - Each one is cut from an approximate region and **tightened to its exact alpha bounding box**.
  - Grass and dirt detail overlays are split into individual sprites, to scatter on the plain grass base.
  - Chimney smoke (6 frames of 48×48) and the cat animation sheet are kept whole for later.
- **`manifest.json`** sits next to the sprites. It lists each sprite's file, size, frame layout (for crops: frame width and height, stage order) and the sampled grass base colour.
- **A contact sheet** of every exported sprite (`_contact.png`, also ignored) is used to check the cuts by eye.

## Files
`tools/art/build_pack_assets.py` (committed), `app/assets/sprites/pack/**` (generated, ignored)

## How we verify it
- The contact sheet shows every sprite cut cleanly, with no neighbour bleed and nothing clipped.
- The crop strips line up their baselines.

## Skills
spritecook-workflow-essentials (manifest conventions only; **no** AI upload of pack art)

## What was built
2026-11-15:
- `tools/art/slice_packs.py` finds sprites and draws contact sheets. `tools/art/build_pack_assets.py` does the build.
- **9 crop strips**, each 5 frames: seed, sprout, young, ripe, dormant.
  - Crops: grape, bean, pepper, cauliflower, peas, lettuce, squash, pumpkin, wheat.
  - The frame height matches each crop row (19–39 px), and baselines line up.
  - Dormant is generated by recolouring greens to olive and tan and muting fruit.
- **26 props, trees and buildings**, cut to their exact edges.
  - Small bits of neighbouring sprites that touch a region's edge are dropped.
  - A stray plank pixel above `rocks_small` remains. It's tiny and not used in v1.
- **Ground details:** 77 grass and 108 dirt overlays.
- **Whole sheets** kept for animation later: smoke (6×48²) and cat (3×18 of 32²).
- **Grass base colour** `#96c55f`, sampled from the pack's grass autotile.
- `manifest.json` and `_contact.png` are written to `app/assets/sprites/pack/`, which is git-ignored.
- **Not used:** bird animations, the interior tileset, walls and floors, and trees in the right half of Plants.png. They're available if we need them later.
