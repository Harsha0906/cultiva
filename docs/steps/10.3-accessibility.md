# Step 10.3: Accessibility pass

## Automated checks (Flutter's accessibility guideline matchers)
Run in widget tests for **Farm** (with the Flame scene off), **Tasks**, **Shop**, **Progress**, **Profile**, **Focus** and **Onboarding**:
- `androidTapTargetGuideline`: every tap target is at least 48 × 48 dp.
- `labeledTapTargetGuideline`: every tap target has a label.
- `textContrastGuideline`: text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text).

## Large text
The same screens are pumped at **text scale 2.0** on a 360 dp-wide phone and must show **no overflow errors**. Anything that breaks is fixed with wrapping, flexible rows or scrolling. Clipping isn't an acceptable fix.

## Manual review list
- **Screen reader:** plants on the Flame farm are announced through the invisible cell buttons from step 4.2 ("Read, ripe, tap to harvest"). The capybara's bubble is a live region.
- **Colour is never the only signal:** plot signs show counts, the nav has a tile plus bold text, the shop uses Owned and Need-more text, and postcards show counts.
- **Reduce Motion** (system or app) disables coin flight, particles, the capybara's walk and the sparkle animation.
- **Every swipe-able action** has a visible button.

## What was built
2026-11-15:
- **`test/a11y_test.dart`:** 16 tests over 8 screens: Farm, Tasks, Shop, Progress, Calendar, Profile, Focus and Onboarding.
  - Each screen runs with the demo farm seeded, on a 360 × 780 dp phone.
  - Each is checked against the tap-target, label and contrast guidelines, and at **2× text** for overflow.
  - Button and panel art is decoded first, so the contrast check sees the real backgrounds.
- **What it found, and the fixes:**
  | Problem | Fix |
  |---|---|
  | Primary (terracotta) and teal button faces gave white labels only 3.7:1 and 3.9:1 | Faces darkened in `draw_ui_kit.py` to `#b5502e` (5.1:1) and `#2a6f70` (5.8:1); the kit was regenerated |
  | Text buttons and field labels in teal on paper were 3.1:1 | The theme's `primary` is now `tealDark` (6.2:1) |
  | Farm plant cells were 39.6 dp wide on a 360 dp phone | Screen-reader cells grow to at least 48 dp (touch still goes through the scene) |
  | The farm scene's tap detector had no label | Hidden from screen readers; the labelled cells cover it |
  | Farm header at 2×: the coin pill and date badge overflowed | The pill wraps onto two lines; the date badge scales to fit its tile |
  | Tasks at 2×: the plot header and task rows overflowed | The header wraps; the action button is capped at 132 dp and its label wraps |
  | Shop at 2×: cards overflowed | One wide card per row at large text |
  | Calendar at 2×: the day badge overflowed | The tiny count badge scales down (the count is also in the cell label and day sheet) |
- **Reviewed by hand:** the capybara bubble is a live region; Reduce motion stops the coin flight, particles, the capybara's walk and the onboarding blink and breathing circle; colour is never the only signal; every swipe has a button.
- **139 tests pass.**
