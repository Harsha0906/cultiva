# Step 10.3: Accessibility pass

## Automated checks (Flutter's accessibility guideline matchers)
Run in widget tests for **Farm** (with the Flame scene off), **Tasks**, **Shop**, **Progress**, **Profile**, **Focus** and **Onboarding**:
- `androidTapTargetGuideline`: every tap target is at least 48 × 48 dp.
- `labeledTapTargetGuideline`: every tap target has a label.
- `textContrastGuideline`: text contrast is at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text).

## Large text
The same screens are pumped at **text scale 2.0** on a 360 dp-wide phone and must show **no overflow errors**. Anything that breaks is fixed with wrapping, flexible rows or scrolling. Clipping isn't an acceptable fix.

## Manual review list
- **Screen reader:** plants on the Flame farm are announced through the invisible cell buttons from step 4.2 ("Read, ripe, tap to harvest"). The capybara's bubble is a live region.
- **Colour is never the only signal:** plot signs show counts, the nav has a tile plus bold text, the shop uses Owned and Need-more text, and postcards show counts.
- **Reduce Motion** (system or app) disables coin flight, particles, the capybara's walk and the sparkle animation.
- **Every swipe-able action** has a visible button.
