# Step 7.2: Barn storage & decoration spots

## Goal
The barn (the founder's sketch) becomes **Storage**. It holds today's harvest and every decoration you own, and from there you place decorations on the farm.

## Farm
- **3 decoration spots** in the bottom strip, between the pond and the path. Centres are x = 82, 118, 154, with the base at y = 286 (art px).
  - Placed items are drawn bottom-centred, after the plots.
  - Empty spots show a faint dashed outline, only when you own something not yet placed.
- **Removed from the background:** the fixed mushroom and bush that sat in the spots.
- **Taps:**
  - A decoration spot opens Storage.
  - The barn opens Storage (it showed a placeholder message before).

## Storage screen (`/barn`, top level)
- **Today's harvest:** a crop thumbnail, name and coins for each harvest today, or "Nothing harvested yet today." The total sits at the top.
- **Decorations:** a grid of owned items, each marked "On the farm · spot 2" or "In storage".
  - **Tap one** to open the spot picker: 3 buttons showing what's in each spot, plus **Take back to storage** if the item is placed.
  - Placing it in an occupied spot **swaps**: the old item goes back to storage.
- **Empty state:** "Visit the shop to decorate your farm", with a button to the Shop tab.

## Rules (`ShopService`)
- `place(item, spot)` runs in a transaction: it clears whatever is in the spot, then sets this item's spot.
- `takeBack(item)` clears its spot.
- `placed()` returns spot → item.
- Spots are 0–2. Placing an item you don't own fails.

## Verification
- Service tests: place, swapping a placed item, taking it back, unowned refused, a bad spot refused.
- Widget test: the barn shows today's harvest and owned items.
- Emulator: buy the well → place it in spot 2 → it appears on the farm.
