# Step 8.2: Weekly chart & calendar of farm postcards

## Weekly chart
- An `fl_chart` bar chart of **tasks done per day for the last 7 days**.
- Today's bar is terracotta and the others teal. Each day is labelled with a letter (M T W T F S S, today last).
- Bar values are written above each bar, so colour isn't the only signal.
- **Summary line**, which is also the screen-reader label: "This week: 23 done · best day Tuesday (6) · 5 of 7 days farmed".

## Calendar ("farm postcards")
- A month grid (Mon-first) with month arrows, which never go past the current month.
- **Each day is a small postcard tile:**
  - The day number.
  - Grass tinted by how much was done: 0 = pale, 1–2, 3–4, then 5+ = rich green.
  - Up to 3 tiny crop sprites from that day's harvest.
  - A mood dot when a mood was logged.
- **Today** has a terracotta border. Future days are blank and can't be tapped.
- **Tapping a day** opens the **day sheet**:
  - the date
  - the auto-log line
  - the list of tasks done, with crop thumbnails
  - the mood
  - the note, editable for any past day too: "Add a note"
- **The header's calendar button** (`/progress?view=calendar`) scrolls straight to the calendar.

## What was built
2026-11-15:
- `week_chart.dart`: an fl_chart bar chart with value labels on top, today in terracotta, and a summary line that is also the screen-reader label.
- `calendar_postcards.dart`: month navigation (never past the current month), postcards tinted by tasks done, a sprout icon with the count, a mood dot, today's terracotta border, and blank, untappable future days.
- `day_sheet.dart`: the day's tasks with crop sprites, the auto-log, and editable mood and note.
- The calendar button scrolls to the postcards. The Progress screen uses a scrolling Column (a lazy `ListView` never built the calendar to scroll to, which a test caught). It also has an error state with *Try again*.
- **Performance bug found on the emulator and fixed:** after a burst of writes (seeding ~30 rows, or a rollover), each live screen replayed a full reload per write and lagged for seconds.
  - New `core/live_query.dart` merges a burst into **one** extra reload, with no timers.
  - Applied to the farm, Progress, shop and Storage providers.
  - 2 unit tests.
- **Checked on the emulator:** Progress loads instantly after seeding; mood and note save; the header calendar opens the postcards; today's postcard opens the day sheet with all 4 tasks.
- **109 tests pass.**
