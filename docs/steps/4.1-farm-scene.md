# Step 4.1: Farm layout & Flame scene

## Goal
Render the approved home layout (founder's sketch, design spec §2.1) as crisp pixel art.

## Design
- **World:** 192 × 288 art px (12 × 18 tiles). It's drawn at the **largest whole number of physical pixels per art pixel** that fits, centred horizontally. The grass colour (`#96c55f`) fills the rest of the screen.
- **Layout** (art px), defined once in `game/farm_layout.dart`:
  | Element | Rect | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | Barn (Storage) | (6, 8) 72×72 | left |
  | Cottage (Home) | (106, 4) 80×80 | right; door at x ≈ 146 |
  | Path | from the door down (138..154, 78..106), right (138..190, 92..106), then the right edge (172..190, 92..288) | cobble tile |
  | Plot A bed | (6, 112) 160×48 | 6 cells, 26 px wide |
  | Plot B bed | (6, 188) 160×48 | 6 cells |
  | Bottom strip | y 244..288 | pond (8, 246) 56×36; 3 decoration spots at x 84 / 112 / 140 |
- **Ground:** the CraftPix grass and dirt detail overlays, scattered with a fixed seed and kept away from beds, path and buildings. Pack trees and bushes frame the top corners.
- **New tile:** a 16 px seamless **cobble** tile (`tiles/cobble.png`) in the packs' beige stone, added to `draw_cultiva16.py`.
- **Rendering:**
  - A Flame `FlameGame` with one root component scaled to the art scale.
  - Every sprite uses `FilterQuality.none`.
  - 9-slice beds and pond are drawn by tiling 16 px pieces, never stretching them.
- **Input:** Flutter's `GestureDetector` turns taps into art coordinates through `FarmLayout`, which returns the plot cell, building or capybara under the finger. Flame only draws.

## Verification
- Unit tests: `FarmLayout` scale picks a whole number and centres, the art↔screen transform round-trips, and hit tests find cells, buildings and empty spots correctly.
- Emulator screenshot of the scene.

## Skills
flutter-performance (pixel-perfect scaling), flutter-responsive-layout

## What was built
2026-11-15:
- `game/farm_layout.dart` holds `FarmLayout` (every position in art px) and `FarmViewport`: a whole number of physical px per art px (5 on a Pixel 7), centred, with art↔screen mapping and hit testing.
- `game/farm_game.dart` draws the static layer **once** into an image: grass, scattered pack details, oak, fern and props, the cobble path, tiled 9-slice beds and pond, barn, cottage. Plants and the capybara are drawn every frame.
- New `tiles/cobble.png` (16 px, seamless, 7 irregular stones).
- **Tests:** 7 layout tests (whole-number scale, centring, round-trip, cells, buildings, capybara priority, cells ≥ 48 dp).
