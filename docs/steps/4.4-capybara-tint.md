# Step 4.4: Capybara helper & day/night tint

## Design
- **Capybara NPC:**
  - Walks the right-hand path between y ≈ 100 and 280 at about 12 art px/s, using the walk frames.
  - Pauses for 2–5 s on the idle and blink frames, and faces the way it's walking (the sprite flips).
  - It's always the mascot, **never the player**.
  - **Tap it:** a speech bubble (Flutter overlay that follows its position) with a context tip, chosen from the farm state:
    - ripe plants waiting: "2 crops are ripe — go harvest!"
    - a dormant plant: "Your Read plant misses you. Even 5 minutes helps."
    - all done: "Full basket! Rest those paws."
    - empty farm: "Tap the soil to plant your first task."
    - otherwise a cozy rotating line
  - The bubble hides after 4 s.
  - **Reduce Motion** on: it stands still by the cottage, with blink only.
- **Day/night tint:** a colour wash over the scene based on local time. It ignores touches.
  | Time | Tint |
  |---|---|
  | 05–08 | warm dawn (orange, 8%) |
  | 08–17 | none |
  | 17–20 | golden evening (orange-pink, 14%) |
  | 20–05 | night (blue, 28%) |

## Verification
- Unit tests: `tintFor(hour)` boundaries, and tip selection for each state.
- Emulator screenshot shows the capybara and the bubble.

## Skills
flutter-animation, flutter-accessibility (reduced motion)

## What was built
2026-11-15:
- **Capybara:** walks the path at 12 px/s, pauses 2–5 s at random, blinks, and flips to face left when walking up. Tapping it or the cottage shows `capyTip()` in a speech bubble that follows it and hides after 4 s.
- **Reduce Motion:** it stays near the cottage, and the sparkle stops animating.
- **Tint:** `tintFor(hour)` gives dawn, day, evening and night washes. The screen re-checks every minute, which also picks up the day rollover.
- **Tests:** tint boundaries and tip choice.
- The Flame scene is switched off in widget tests through `farmSceneEnabledProvider`, because its game loop never settles.
