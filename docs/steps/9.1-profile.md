# Step 9.1: Profile

## Goal
The player sees who they are on the farm, and what they've grown over time.

## Screen (`/profile`, opened from the header avatar)
- **Portrait:** the chosen animal avatar at 6× in a paper frame.
- **Name:** the farmer name (default "Farmer"), with an **Edit** button that opens a small dialog (1–24 characters).
- **"Farming since 1 Nov 2026":** the earliest day with a planted task or any coins.
- **Lifetime stats** (4 tiles), all computed from the records, nothing stored twice:
  | Tile | Meaning |
  |---|---|
  | **Days farmed** | Days with at least one completion |
  | **Crops harvested** | Total harvested completions |
  | **Focus hours** | Total minutes from completions ÷ 60, one decimal |
  | **Best day** | Most tasks completed on one day |
- **Choose your avatar:** a grid of the **8 animals × 3 colours**. The selection has a teal border plus a check mark, and choosing one saves immediately.
  - The capybara is **not** offered; that's enforced in the domain since step 2.4.
- **Settings** sit below (step 9.2).

## Data
- `StatsService.lifetime()` returns a `Lifetime` with firstDay, daysFarmed, harvested, focusMinutes, coinsEarned and bestDayCount.
- `SettingsRepository`: farmer name (max 24 characters) and avatar, both existing.

## Verification
- Service test: lifetime totals across 2 days.
- Widget test: picking the owl saves `owl_*`; renaming saves the trimmed name.
