# Step 8.1: Progress → Today card (auto-log, mood, note)

## Goal
Each day is recorded automatically. The player adds how they felt and one line about it, which gives "everyday records" without journaling homework.

## Data (`data/stats_service.dart`)
`DaySummary(day)` holds:
- **done:** tasks completed (ripe or harvested), with titles and crops
- **harvested**
- **focusMinutes:** completed focus minutes plus today's in-progress focus time
- **coins:** what the daily cap counts (harvest, bonus, unharvest); purchases are excluded
- **mood**, **note** from the journal

Methods: `summary(day)`, `week(endingOn)` (7 days, oldest first), `month(year, month)`.

## Card (top of the Progress tab)
- **Title:** "Today · Mon 16 Nov".
- **Auto-log line:** `5 harvested · 50 focus min · 62 coins`, all live.
- **Mood:** 5 labelled pixel chips, **Rough · Meh · Okay · Good · Great**. These are text labels, not emoji, so they read the same on every phone and work with screen readers. Tapping one saves it immediately (tap the selected chip again to clear it).
- **Note:** a one-line field, "How did today go?", up to 140 characters. It saves when you press Done or leave the field, then shows a quiet "Saved".
- **Empty day:** "Nothing logged yet. Your first harvest will show up here."

## Verification
- Service tests:
  - the summary counts done, harvested, focus minutes and coins
  - purchases don't count as coins earned
  - mood and note come through
  - `week` gives 7 days ending today
- Widget test: tap a mood and it's saved; type a note and press Done and it's saved.
