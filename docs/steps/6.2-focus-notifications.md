# Step 6.2: Focus keeps working in the background

## Decision: notifications, not a foreground service
- The roadmap planned an Android **foreground service**. Because the timer is **timestamp-based** (6.1), nothing has to keep running in the background, so a service isn't needed. That avoids the foreground-service permission types Android 14 requires, and the battery cost.
- The founder-visible behaviour is the same: a live countdown in the notification shade, and a "done" alert.

## Notifications (flutter_local_notifications 23)
- **On start or resume:** an **ongoing** notification, "Read is growing 🌱", with a system **countdown chronometer** to the end time. Android counts it down even if the app is killed. It can't be swiped away while the timer runs.
- **At the end time:** a scheduled **"Read is ripe! Tap to harvest."** notification with the **same id**, so it replaces the countdown. It uses `inexactAllowWhileIdle`, so it may arrive a few minutes late under Doze, and needs no exact-alarm permission.
- **On pause, give up or finish in-app:** both are cancelled.
- **Tapping either:** opens Cultiva at `/focus/:taskId`. This works from a cold start (launch details) and while the app is running (the response callback).
- **Permission:** `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` is requested the first time the player presses **Start**, not at launch. If it's denied, the timer still works, with no notifications.
- **Android manifest:** `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, and the plugin's `ScheduledNotificationReceiver` and `ScheduledNotificationBootReceiver`.
- **Testing:** a `notificationsProvider` with a **fake** implementation, so tests never touch the platform.

## Files
`app/lib/core/notifications.dart`, `main.dart` (init and launch-route handling), `app.dart` (taps → router), `AndroidManifest.xml`

## Verification
- Widget test with the fake: Start schedules an ongoing notification and a done notification, and Pause cancels them.
- Emulator: start a 1-minute focus, background the app, see the countdown in the shade, then see "ripe", tap it, and land on Focus.

## Skills
flutter-notifications, flutter-background-execution

## What was built
2026-11-15:
- `core/notifications.dart`: `AppNotifier` (renamed to avoid Riverpod's `Notifier`), `LocalNotifier`, `NoopNotifier` (the default) and `FakeNotifier`.
- `LocalNotifier` creates two channels: `focus_timer` (low, ongoing, countdown chronometer) and `focus_done` (high). The ripe alert reuses the same id as the countdown, so it replaces it.
- **Opening from a notification:** `main.dart` initialises the notifier and opens `launchRoute` on a cold start. `app.dart` listens to `taps` and calls `router.push` while the app is running.
- **Manifest:** POST_NOTIFICATIONS, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and VIBRATE permissions, plus the two plugin receivers. The app label is now **Cultiva**.
- **Checked on the emulator:** after pressing Start, Android asked for permission (allowed). With the app in the background, the shade showed *Breathe is growing* with a live countdown (00:43).
- **Bug found on the emulator and fixed:** in the background, the Focus screen's 1 s timer kept ticking. At the end time it "finished in-app" and **cancelled its own scheduled ripe alert** (Android's alarm log showed `alarm_cancelled`). Now the timer stops when the app leaves the foreground and reloads on resume. The cancel only happens while the player is actually looking at the result.
- **Re-verified end to end on the emulator:** start a 1-minute Breathe session at 11:25:46 and go Home. The shade shows *Breathe is growing* with a countdown. *Breathe is ripe! Tap to harvest your coins.* arrived at 11:27:33 (inexact alarm, about 47 s late). Tapping it opens Cultiva on **Focus → Ready to harvest!**.
