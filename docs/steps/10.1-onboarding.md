# Step 10.1: Onboarding (first harvest in about 60 seconds)

## Goal
A brand-new player understands the loop (plant → grow → harvest → coins) and has a real task planted before they see anything else. There's no sign-up, ever.

## When it shows
- **Trigger:** first launch, when the `onboarded` setting is false. `CultivaApp` checks it at startup and goes to `/onboarding`.
- **Flags:** finishing or skipping sets `onboarded = true`. Debug `/dev/kit` routes are never redirected.

## Steps (one screen, 5 steps, a progress dots row, Back allowed)
1. **Welcome:**
   - The capybara (big, idle and blinking) beside a sprout.
   - "Grandma left you a little farm."
   - "Every task you finish grows a crop. Harvest crops for coins and make the farm yours."
   - Button **Let's go**.
2. **You:**
   - "What should the capybara call you?" Name field (optional, defaults to "Farmer").
   - A row of the **8 animals** (colour 1) to pick your avatar from. More colours are in Profile.
   - Button **Next**.
3. **First daily task:**
   - "What's one thing you want to do every day?"
   - Choice chips: **Read · Walk · Stretch · Meditate**, or type your own.
   - Duration chips: **5 / 15 / 30 min**.
   - It plants a real **Daily** task (every day, random crop) into Plot A.
   - Button **Plant it**.
4. **Your first harvest:**
   - "Take one calm minute. Watch it grow."
   - A **60-second** guided breathing moment, with the crop you just planted growing seed → sprout → young → ripe.
   - Then **Harvest!**: coin burst, the harvest sound and a haptic, plus a **20-coin welcome gift**.
     - Recorded as a `refund` ledger row with refId `welcome`, so it never counts toward the daily cap.
   - "That's how every task works."
   - Skippable at any time: *Skip for now*.
   - The real task isn't completed by this; it's a practice harvest.
5. **Gentle nudge:**
   - "Want one gentle nudge a day?"
   - A time chip (default 8:30 AM), then **Yes, remind me** (asks permission and schedules) or **Not now**.
   - Then **Go to my farm**.

`app/lib/features/onboarding/onboarding_screen.dart`, plus changes to `app.dart` and `router.dart`

## Verification
- **Widget tests:** fresh install → onboarding shows; walking every step plants the task, pays the welcome coins once, sets the avatar and name, sets onboarded, and lands on the farm.
- **Skip:** sets onboarded and plants nothing.
- **The breathing moment** is configurable down to 2 s in tests.
- **Emulator:** the full first-launch flow, after clearing app data.

## What was built
2026-11-15:
- `features/onboarding/onboarding_screen.dart`: one screen with 5 steps, a step-dots bar (announced as "Step n of 5"), Back on steps 2–3 and **Skip** on steps 1–3.
  1. Welcome: the capybara (`CapybaraSprite`, idle blink, still when Reduce motion is on) and a sprout.
  2. Name plus the 8 animals (colour 1). Picking one or pressing Next closes the keyboard.
  3. Read · Walk · Stretch · Meditate · Something else, with 5/15/30 min. Plants a real Daily task, every day, random crop.
  4. Breathing minute: the circle breathes 4 s in / 4 s out with a countdown and the crop grows seed → ripe. **Harvest!** plays sound and haptics, flies coins to the gift pill (instant with Reduce motion), and pays **20 coins once** (`refund` row, refId `welcome`, so a replay never pays twice). The real task is not completed.
  5. Reminder: an 8:30 AM time chip, **Yes, remind me** (permission, then schedule) or **Not now**.
- **Redirect:** `buildRouter(onboarded:)` adds an async redirect to `/onboarding` until the setting is true (checked once, then cached). `/dev/*` is never redirected. The dev kit gets a **Replay onboarding** button.
- **Landed early from 10.2**, because onboarding plays sounds: `core/sfx.dart`, `tools/audio/make_sfx.py` and `assets/sfx/*.wav`.
- **Tests:** full walk (name, avatar, task, gift, sounds, reminder, onboarded, lands in the shell); Skip (nothing planted, 0 coins); the gift is paid once on replay; a fresh launch redirects. Existing navigation tests seed `onboarded = true`. **120 tests pass.**
- **Checked on the emulator** after clearing app data: all 5 steps, the Android permission prompt, and landing on the farm with the owl, 20 coins and "Next up: Read · 15 min". The daily alarm was registered. The first run showed the gift label overflowing a tiny panel and the text "Your peas is ripe"; both fixed (a coin pill, and "It's ripe! Tap Harvest.").
